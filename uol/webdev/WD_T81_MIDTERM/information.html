ParcPark - Information

Thank you for your interest in ParcPark! We hope you will enjoy your trip. We recommend wearing comfortable, quick-drying clothes- some of the fun attractions have splashing water. As the day of fun goes by and the evening cools off, you might want to have a sweater handy. Comfortable shoes are the key to long lasting fun as you walk around the park. Remember to stay hydrated and eat a good breakfast or enjoy a meal in one of our park concessions. If you have any questions please contact us!

Driving directions

From the North

If you are North from our park, simply head South - follow your compass and you will not miss it. Discovering the magic starts with discovering directions.

From the South

Similar to coming from the North but opposite! So just go up, not down. Our park will be on the right as you pass Mountain of Wisdom. Just follow the trail.

From the East

Turn left, turn right, take 3 steps back, clap your hands, stand on one foot for 5 seconds and then roll forward 10 times. You know there are never too many times to roll. After that, you should be able to see our entrance.

From The West

Turn right, turn left...just kidding go left...I mean right?? You will find us. Good luck!
