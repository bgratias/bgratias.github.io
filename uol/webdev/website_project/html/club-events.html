Club Events

September

Sat September 18

Membership Drive

Time: 10:00am

Place: IAPM Mall on HuiHai Lu

Come and see what the Shanghai Astronomy Club is all about. Meet some of our members, get your questions answered, and see what the club can do for you.

Sat September 25

Astro Photography Class

Time: 9:00am

Place: We-Work, JingAn Office Space

You will learn about stargazing and photo techniques during daytime presentations, and observe and photograph the sky with guidance from the instructors at night. Topics include The World At Night, photography, stargazing, celestial phenomena, step by step guides to nightscapes, astrophotography and image processing.

Sun September 26

Equipment Swap Meet

Time: 10:00am

Place: Guilin Park

Welcome all clubs and members to share in this year's equipment swap meet. Come and find a deal on used equipment. We will provide soft drinks,water and chips for all and have a grill for burgers. The event is free to attend!

October

Sat October 2

Night Under the Stars Party

Time: 5:00pm

Place: SheShan Mountain Observatory

Sheshan is one of Shanghai's best kept secrets. In October, we will be holding our annual Night Under the Stars Star Party. Please note that no flash photos are allowed to protect everyone's night vision. Please bring a red flashlight or for a small donation, mini red flashlights will be available for purchase at the door.
