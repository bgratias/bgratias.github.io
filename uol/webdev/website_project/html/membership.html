Become a Shanghai
Astronomy Club Member

Make new friends

Attend club events, participate in club activities, including the annual members only gala. Our club welcomes men and women, from all ages, and from all walks of life. Astronomers are good people!

See the stars

Gain full access to club owned equimpment including a range of telescopes and astro imaging hardware. All free for members to use. Members are also welcome to use our club's dark sky observing site conveniently located on the outskirts of Shanghai.

Learn something new

Astronomy is a positive, relaxing and beautiful hobby which can keep you engaged for a lifetime. Attend our classes, workshops and monthly club meetings featuring visiting speakers. Learn from people who are passionate about the sky.

Give back to your community

Happiness comes from giving. The Shanghai Astronomy Club offers many opportunities to be a contributor in your community by sharing your love of the stars with others. We are also active promoters of preserving a dark sky environment.
